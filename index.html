Questions worth asking

Is the data real?

No. Every market, provider, asset and figure is fictional. The prototype is not connected to live coverage, market-share or regulatory data.

Does the AI analyst use a live model?

Not in this prototype. Its answers are scripted from the demo dataset and labeled as preliminary.

Can it use our own data?

A production version would import your records. This concept shows how that data could be organized and presented.

Who is it designed for?

Telecom consultants, infrastructure advisors and business development teams who explain local markets to clients.

What would you build next?

A policy research timeline and a client pipeline, shaped by what proves most useful in real engagements.
